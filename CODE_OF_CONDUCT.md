# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

DenDenAPI is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Community members are expected to:

- communicate with empathy, patience, and respect;
- give constructive feedback focused on the work;
- accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes;
- respect differing viewpoints and levels of experience;
- protect private information and the safety of other participants.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following behavior is not tolerated:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language;
- personal attacks, insults, trolling, or deliberately disruptive conduct;
- unwanted sexual attention or imagery;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- sustained disruption of project discussions or moderation decisions.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project, including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and related community channels.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Enforcement decisions will consider context, severity, repetition, and the safety of affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through a private contact method listed on their GitHub profile. Do not publish sensitive reports in an issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible by a solo-maintainer project.
